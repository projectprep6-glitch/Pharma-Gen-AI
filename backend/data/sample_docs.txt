Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) combines a retrieval system with a generative model.
The retriever finds relevant documents and the generator conditions on them to produce answers.

Embeddings convert text into dense vectors so semantically similar passages are near each other.
Common embedding models include sentence-transformers like all-MiniLM-L6-v2.

A vector index (FAISS, Annoy, or sklearn NearestNeighbors) stores vectors for fast similarity search.
The retriever issues nearest-neighbour queries over the index to get candidate passages.

In the RAG pattern, retrieved passages are passed as context to the LLM which then composes
an answer grounded in the retrieved evidence. This reduces hallucinations and improves factuality.
